# Phase 2: New features and enhancements

> Companion to `docs/handoff.md`. Agreed 2026-10-12, right after Phase 1.5. The open ideas from the **Phase 2 backlog** (handoff, section 13) stay listed there; this file holds the items the user has picked and their agreed decisions.

>
> **Direction (user, 2026-10-12):** the game is closer to Way of the Samurai (Acquire), Shinobido and Rise of Ronin in its main quest and game design, with Sekiro's combat. The current arenas are placeholder and testing maps; when the real game flow is designed, they stay in the project but are not reachable by players.

## 1. Rules for this phase

- **New features, one item at a time.** Each is built, tested by the user, and logged before the next (work agreement, sections 1 to 7). Full check before each build, then an explicit go; the build-log entry is appended without reading the file.
- **Decisions below were agreed on 2026-10-12.** Anything an item does not settle gets a proposed default and one question at that item's full check.
- **New menus are scenes with one shared Theme** (user, 2026-10-12): screens are built from `Control` nodes (containers and anchors for the layout, `Button` focus for keyboard and gamepad navigation), the look (fonts, colors, button styles) lives in one `Theme` resource (`ui_theme.tres`), and scripts hold only behavior. Texts, colors and the kanji stay `@export` values or Theme entries the user can tune in the editor. System serif fonts for now; a bundled free-license font is a later polish. The death and victory screens became scenes in item 4; the HUDs stay code-drawn until real art exists.
- **Placeholders stay marked.** The test arenas, the default start arena and the empty Options panels are placeholders and are logged as such.
- **Verify by playing.** Each item ends with a short "How to verify" that also covers what it touches (fight, guard and deflect, lock-on, death and resurrection, scene reload).

### 8. Generic interact system

- **First interactables (proposed):** a door (can be locked by a flag), a chest, a switch, and a placeholder NPC that shows one line of text. A crowd NPC is also an interactable with one or two generic lines from a pool (chosen by reputation in Phase 4).

- **The shared `interact` button (user's rule, proposed order):** in the air a ledge grab (exactly as today); on the ground the nearest `Interactable` in front; on the ground with nothing to interact with, the wall hug (item 9). Interactables work on the ground in Locomotion or Crouch only.

### 9. Wall hug
- **Status:** **built and tested 2026-10-16** (what was built: `docs/built-reference.md`, "Wall hug"). The text below is the plan as discussed. Decisions made at the full check: a single press, an automatic latched peek at a wall end (no button), attack, guard, dodge and jump just leave the hug and act normally, the stealth kill from the hug pulls the target in over 0.15 s (a cheap glide), the stealth numbers are Player exports (not `EnemyAIData`), a slim 0.2 m body and no personal space push while hugging, the demo wall in `test_arena`.
- **What (user):** the Sekiro and Tenchu move. Press `interact` while facing a wall, on the ground, and the character presses her back to it. Called "wall hug" so it is not confused with the ledge shimmy (hanging from a ledge).
- **Entry and exit (user, proposed single press):** `interact` facing a wall within reach, in any stance. Leave with `interact` again, moving away from the wall, a jump or a dodge. **Open:** a single press (default) or the double press some Sekiro PC players describe.
- **Sidle:** slow and quiet along the wall. **Peek around a corner** by shifting the camera.
- **Stealth bonus:** enemies see the player more slowly and hear almost nothing (the awareness numbers).
- **Reuse:** `LedgeSensor.find_wall` (wall jump uses it); the shared button order is in item 8, so this item comes after it.
- **Not included in the first version:** an ambush attack from the hug when a guard walks past (Tenchu style).

### 11. Item system and throwables
- **Status:** **11a and 11b built and tested 2026-10-19** (what was built: `docs/built-reference.md`, "Item system and throwables, 11a" and "Hold to aim, 11b"). At the 11b full check: the 0.2 s hold threshold (a tap throws on release), free aim from a lock (the camera swings onto the target's chest first, the lock comes back on release), and the throw solved to the crosshair's aim point. **11c (added after the 11b test, same day):** attack throws from the aim and the aim goes on (0.3 s recovery), releasing `use_item` never throws, and the Options "AIM MODE" STICKY (default) / HOLD decides whether letting go ends the aim; guard and heal end it in both modes. **11d (same day, from the 11a test):** a guard that hears a noise or loses a glimpse of the player now walks to the spot, looks around for `spot_check_time` (30 s) and then returns to its route or post; 3 noises in one check make it search (alert, still not knowing about the player). Discussed 2026-10-16; **redesigned 2026-10-19** (the user: items work like Tenchu and Nightreign, so the throw is the first item of a general item system). Decisions made at the full check: the defaults below stand (ammo set and counts, no sword draw, keys F and G with D-pad Right and Left); after the test the user set the shuriken and kunai noise to 10 m (the metal throwables are the accurate distraction tool, the rock and ceramic the loud wide lure). The text below is the plan as discussed.
- **What (user):** a Sekiro-style prosthetic idea done as a **normal throw with selectable ammo**: a rock, a shuriken, a kunai, broken ceramic; each lethal or non-lethal. No player bow (yumi). Later items (Tenchu style: onigiri, rice to mark a spot, and so on) use the same system.
- **The item system (user, 2026-10-19):**
  - The **gourd keeps its own quick button** (`heal`, Q) and is not part of the item list (Nightreign style: the flask is quick, other items use other buttons).
  - **One selected item** at a time. `cycle_item` steps through everything carried; `use_item` does what the selected item's kind says (a throwable starts the throw action; later kinds start their own state, for example eating or placing). *(proposed)* A single list now; categories on separate buttons can be split out later without breaking anything.
  - `ItemData` (base resource: name, icon shape and color, count, kind). `ThrowableData` extends it. `ItemBelt` holds the list, the selected index and the counts (in memory for now). In 11a it only contains throwables.
  - **Input Map:** `use_item` (F, controller D-pad Right) and `cycle_item` (G, controller D-pad Left) *(proposed keys)*. They replace the earlier names `throw` and `cycle_ammo`. Both appear in the Options controls pages.
- **`ThrowableData` (proposed):** damage, posture, a lethal flag, noise, `speed` and `gravity` per ammo (count lives in the belt). Rocks and ceramic arc a little and are non-lethal; shuriken and kunai fly straight and fast and are lethal. Default: **small damage and a short stun, not a killing tool**, so close-range stealth stays the focus. Starting counts 10 each (placeholder).
- **Noise at a position (proposed):** `Player.get_noise_radius()` is centered on the player, so a landing rock needs a world noise event. A new `EnemyAwareness.hear_noise_at(position, radius)` reuses the `hear_shout` fields (the enemy turns suspicious and investigates the spot; no line of sight needed). A rock can lure a guard from his post.
- **Throwing is an attack:** a short throw action (`data/actions/player/throw.tres`, an `ActionData`) with a recovery, following the usual action rules. `ThrowState` is the state for throwable items; allowed from Locomotion, Crouch and Dash, walk-only during the action, dodge, jump and guard cancel it after the locked window, no throwing in the air, and throwing does not draw the sword *(proposed)*.
- **11a:** the tap throw (the camera's forward direction, or toward the locked target's chest when locked on), `ItemData`, `ThrowableData`, `ItemBelt`, the noise at a position, `cycle_item`, and the **item HUD**. Two new Input Map actions as editor steps.
- **Item HUD (user, 2026-10-19):** like Sekiro, **bottom right**, in the prosthetic slot. A code-drawn square icon per item (grey rock, silver star, dark kunai, white shard), the name, and the count; dimmed at 0; a short flash on cycling. Margin, size and colors are `@export` values. The gourd stays bottom left under the health bar for now. No button prompts yet (backlog).
- **11b (user, proposed shape accepted):** hold `use_item` to aim, for throwables only. The camera moves to head height with the head hidden (not a real first person), a crosshair shows for shuriken and kunai, and a **dotted arc preview with a landing marker** (grenade style) for rocks and ceramic. Releasing throws. While aiming: slow walk (like the guard's forced walk), the lock-on suspended, the spring arm shortened in tight spaces. The preview simulates the projectile's own gravity.
- **Not included:** any non-throwable item (onigiri, marking rice and so on), pickups, saving (ammo counts are saved in Phase 4), enemy throwers, a real throw animation, moving the gourd to a bottom-right quick slot.
- **Reuse:** the projectile system is reused by enemy archers, revolvers and rifles in Phase 5; the item system is reused by the Phase 4 and 5 items.
- **Needs:** item 10's lethal flag, so this comes after it.

### 12a. Navigation (the navmesh)
- **Status:** done and tested 2026-10-19 (discussed 2026-10-16); **split into 12a and 12b on 2026-10-19** (the user: the navmesh first, the links as a follow-up). 12a is walking on flat levels only. The hub of Phase 4 and the street encounters are its users (`docs/phase-4-6-notes.md`, section 5).
- **What (user):** a navmesh system for enemies. **Rule (proposed):** a per-enemy `use_navigation` flag, **off by default**, so every existing arena and preset behaves exactly as before (the same rule as `use_awareness`).
- **Shape (proposed):** `NavigationRegion3D` for the baked walkable area, `NavigationAgent3D` on the enemy, the change mostly in the enemy's "walk toward a spot" code, plus baking the arenas that use it.
- **Limits to know:** a navmesh handles walking around walls and stalls but **not climbing**. A ground enemy cannot follow onto a roof unless jump or climb links are added; the existing "from another level it stands and watches, still alert" rule covers it. Roofs get their own walkable area so rooftop guards can patrol. In Phase 4 and 5 the streamed world needs one region per cell with matching edges. Jump, drop and climb links are item 12b.

### 12b. Navigation links (follow-up to 12a)
- **Status:** split on 2026-10-19 into **12b-1a** (the drop crossing, one hand-placed link: done and tested 2026-10-19), **12b-1b** (an editor tool that generates drop links: done and tested 2026-10-19), **12b-2** (jump: done and tested 2026-10-19) and **12b-3** (climb, going up from the ground to a platform: done and tested 2026-10-20).
- **12b-1a as built (agreed 2026-10-19):** per-enemy `use_nav_links` (off by default, needs `use_navigation`); a one-way `NavigationLink3D` on navigation layer 2, which only enemies with the switch can path over; the enemy walks to the edge, pauses (`nav_link_pause` 0.3 s), steps off (`nav_drop_step_speed` 2.0), falls and recovers (`nav_link_recovery` 0.4 s); no fall damage; a drop longer than `nav_drop_max_height` (6 m) is not used; a target up to that far below counts as reachable; the edge guard is off only while crossing. Every state that walks uses it (patrol, noise and spot checks, searches, body walks, chase). Not yet: a way back up (an enemy that dropped stays below), the other enemy types and NPCs.
- **12b-3 as built (agreed 2026-10-19):** one switch, `use_nav_links`, covers all three link kinds; a climb link is a `NavigationLink3D` on navigation layer value 8 (the fourth checkbox), usually bidirectional, from the ground at the foot of a wall to the top of the platform; `nav_climb_max_height` in the `ai` preset (6.0 m, 0 = this enemy type never climbs and its agent does not get layer 8) and `nav_climb_speed` (2.0 m/s); two legs (straight up or down at the foot of the wall, then horizontal at the top) with gravity off; pause and recovery reuse the drop numbers; `can_reach_height` also counts a target up to `nav_climb_max_height` above the enemy as reachable. Climb links are hand-placed (a ladder or wall asset can carry its own link later); a guard that dropped can now return by climbing.
- **12b-2 as built (agreed 2026-10-19):** one switch, `use_nav_links`, covers drops and jumps (no separate jump switch); a jump link is a `NavigationLink3D` on navigation layer value 4 (the third checkbox), usually bidirectional, across a gap; `nav_jump_max_distance` in the `ai` preset (5.0 m, 0 = this enemy type never jumps and its agent does not get layer 4), `nav_jump_max_rise` (1.5 m), `nav_jump_speed` (5.0 m/s); the arc is computed so the jump ends on the exit, no steering in the air; pause and recovery reuse the drop numbers; a jump that falls 3.5 m below the exit is given up and re-pathed. The first links are hand-placed, a jump generator is not planned yet.
- **12b-1b as built (2026-10-19):** a `@tool` helper node in the arena with an `@export_tool_button` ("Generate drop links") that scans the baked navmesh edges, casts rays down just past each edge and adds `NavigationLink3D` nodes (layer 2, one-way) where the ground is between a step and `nav_drop_max_height` below; spacing and heights are settings on the node; pressing it again clears only the generated links, never hand-placed ones. Generated in the editor and saved in the scene, not at runtime.
- **What (user, proposed order):** enemies that cross between levels with `NavigationLink3D` links: first a **drop** (roof to ground), then a **jump** over a gap, then a **climb** last. The navmesh side is only the links; the real work is an enemy state per link kind (timing, movement, later animation).
- **Rule (proposed):** a per-enemy switch (off by default, like `use_navigation`), so only chosen enemies follow across levels.
- **Open (the user's design choice):** if enemies can follow onto roofs, the roofs stop being a guaranteed escape (`docs/phase-4-6-notes.md`, section 5). Decide at the 12b full check which enemies get links.
- **Needed by:** the Phase 4 hub (rooftop guards, hunters that follow, the roof escape).

### 13. Ledge enhancements (agreed 2026-10-20; split into 13a, 13b, 13c, built and tested one at a time)

- **Dropped by the user (already works):** a hang drop to a lower ledge (drop, then hold `interact` or `jump` near the lower ledge: `find_ledge()` grabs it) and a ledge-to-ledge jump (jump, then hold `interact`). Neither needs new code.
- **13a. Corner shimmy:** while hanging, left or right at the end of the ledge turns around a **square corner**: inside (concave, the ledge turns toward you) and outside (convex, the ledge wraps away). 90 degrees only; any other angle keeps today's behavior (the shimmy stops). Default: a scripted blend of position and facing of about 0.35 s (`corner_turn_time`), the ledge height stays continuous, and the shimmy stops if the body would not fit after the turn. `LedgeSensor` gets the corner probe, `LedgeHangState` the turn. A corner station (an L-shaped and a box-shaped ledge) is added to `movement_yard` (editor steps, the scene is not shipped).
- **13b. Low-ceiling climb (no separate fast climb; [x] done and tested 2026-10-20):** the normal climb stays the default and `ledge_climb.tres` is not touched. When the top has room to crouch but not to stand, the climb is the **low variant** and ends in Crouch (`data/actions/player/ledge_climb_low.tres`, a copy of the normal climb with a longer placeholder duration, tuned by the user). If even the crouch capsule does not fit, the grab is refused as today. `LedgeSensor` gets a "fits crouching" check; `LedgeClimbState` picks the variant when it starts.
- **13c. Hang hit reactions ([x] done and tested 2026-10-20):** a hit while hanging no longer drops you at once. **Hit 1:** a flinch, the character swings to one side (random left or right) and keeps hanging. **Hit 2:** a flinch to the opposite side (so the order can be left then right, or right then left). **Hit 3:** knocked off, as `knock_off()` does today. Placeholder: the swing is the existing hit wobble plus an arm jolt. ### 15. Carrying unconscious bodies (picked 2026-10-20)

- **Source:** the "Non-lethal extras" backlog entry (item 10 made enemies unconscious; item 7e has guards carry bodies). - **Agreed (2026-10-21):**
  - **Carry as an overlay, not a state:** heal, jump, crouch and lock-on must keep working while carrying, so the player holds a "carried body" that `Locomotion`, `Crouch` and `Air` read (slow-down, rules); there is no `CarryState`. Heal uses the left hand and the carry the right, so heal does **not** drop the body.

Proposed, to confirm at the 15a check: `use_item` aims a body throw (15c); wall jump and ledge grab start but hanging drops the body.
  - **Wake timer:** unconscious bodies keep counting while carried. If one wakes mid-carry the player drops it and it wakes where it lies. Dead bodies never wake. Bosses are not carried.

Guards cannot discover a hidden body by sight (one condition in the body discovery); noise and the wake timer are unaffected; the body stays visible to the player. One body per spot (`hiding_capacity` 1). The player cannot hide in a cover volume (a separate feature).

Solid containers (crate, closet) use the same script with `player_enters` off: the body is placed straight into the volume. Placeholder bush, crate and closet in `test_arena`.

  - **Throwing (15c, proposed):** while carrying, hold `use_item` to aim (reusing `AimState` and the arc preview), release to throw; about 5 m at most; the landing makes a noise of about 8 m; an unconscious body is not hurt; a body thrown into a fall zone falls; no damage to guards.
  - **Guard reactions (15d):** a guard that sees the player carrying a body counts as discovering it and shows the `corpse_reaction`; the player's sight fill is faster while carrying (proposed 1.5 times). A body that wakes by its timer now wakes suspicious, not calm at its post (a change to item 10's wake).
- **15a as built (2026-10-21):** the body hangs over the right shoulder (not across the arms): head behind, tilted 25 degrees head-down, middle 0.3 m to the right and 1.35 m high (0.95 m crouched); guards carry the same way (`corpse_carry_shoulder`, `corpse_carry_tilt_degrees`; `corpse_carry_height` now means the body's middle). Guards ignore a carried body until 15d.
- **15b as built (2026-10-21):** `CoverVolume` (an `Area3D` in the group `cover` with one box shape; no new physics layer) and a `CoverPrompt` child ("Hide body"). A lying body is hidden when 3 of 5 points along its length are inside a volume (`CoverVolume.MIN_POINTS`; any body lying there counts, however it got there); guards cannot discover a hidden body by sight, even from beside it. `CoverPlacement` finds the best spot (a grid of body spots, 16 facings, most points inside, least walking, then the checks of `Player.cover_spot_valid`); `CoverPlaceState` glides there (0.3 s, stance kept, dodge, jump or pushing away cancels). `player_enters` true for a bush (the body is laid with the player at its edge), false for a solid container (crate, closet: the body goes straight in); `capacity` 1. Placeholder props in `scenes/props/` (`hiding_bush`, `hiding_crate`, `hiding_closet`). **Fit tweak (2026-10-21, user):** `shrink_body` on a volume scales the body to fit its longest side (95 percent, 0.5 to 1; a placeholder for folding it); on for the crate and closet, off for the bush (the player is in a hurry and just drops it); pick-up or waking restores the size.

### 23. Witness and relay (proposed 2026-10-26, not started)
